What's in your freezer? I didn't like any of the myriad apps out there. So there's this thing.

It's made for the folks I live with who are terminally unaware of what's in the freezer. Who also hate to type. Presents you with a bunch of images of the things in your freezer. Uses the built-in barcode scanners available in most mobile browsers to easily add/remove stuff from the freezer. (Don't search for it, scan it!)

Asks you to take a picture of the thing you're putting in the freezer. Uses a google login to figure out who can access the freezer. We also plan on expanding it to cover what's in your pantry...and maybe what's also in your inside freezer vs your outside freezer.
